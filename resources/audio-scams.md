# Audio & plugin scams

## Red flags

- “FL Studio / Ableton full version” via Telegram, Discord, or cracked `.exe` / `.dmg`  
- Sample packs sold on random sites with no license text  
- “Royalty-free” claims with no link to terms of use  
- USB “studio bundles” that are rebranded free software  
- “Mastering for $5” that asks for your DAW project + payment before any contract  
- Fake “Spitfire / Native Instruments” download pages not on the vendor domain  

## Safer habits

1. Download only from the vendor domain in [README.md](../README.md).  
2. For samples, keep a screenshot or PDF of the license per project.  
3. For AI music/voice, use [ai-catalog](https://github.com/Jay-Lokhande/ai-catalog) official links only.  
4. Education discounts: complete checkout on **ableton.com**, **avid.com**, **steinberg.net**, **adobe.com**, etc. — not third-party “key resellers.”  
5. If a deal needs crypto or wire transfer only, treat it as a scam.

## If you already downloaded something suspicious

- Do not run unsigned installers; scan with your OS vendor’s guidance and delete unknown binaries.  
- Rotate passwords if you entered them on a non-official store page.
