## 2 — Safety defaults

1. Download from the **vendor domain** in the guide — not Telegram “cracks.”  
2. Save a **license screenshot** for every sample pack you use in a shipped project.  
3. Read [audio-scams.md](audio-scams.md) before paying anyone who is not on an official store.
