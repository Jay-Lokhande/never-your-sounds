# Sound Student

**Music production, audio for games, and podcast tooling for students** — DAWs, notation, legal sample libraries, and middleware docs. No pirated plugins or cracked-software links.

> **Disclaimer:** Student pricing and license terms change on vendor sites only.

> **Scams:** Fake “full version” DAW cracks and stolen sample packs → [resources/audio-scams.md](resources/audio-scams.md)

You can [navigate](NAVIGATION.md), find [Hub](https://jay-lokhande.github.io/student-dev-catalogs/) (all catalogs), and browse [Sister repos](resources/sister-repos.md).
