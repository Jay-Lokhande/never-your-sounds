# Accessibility — captions, transcripts, clarity

Audio projects should be usable for deaf and hard-of-hearing audiences and for viewers in noisy environments.

Back: [README](../README.md) · [design-for-devs](https://github.com/Jay-Lokhande/design-for-devs)

## Practices

1. **Captions** on published video (not auto-only if you can review them).  
2. **Transcripts** for podcasts — many hosts provide transcript fields on official sites.  
3. **Dialog intelligibility** — high-pass noise, level speech above music beds in mixes.  
4. **Visual UI** — if your app has audio controls, follow WCAG contrast guidance via [design-for-devs](https://github.com/Jay-Lokhande/design-for-devs).
